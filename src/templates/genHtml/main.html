Cardano Blockchain Hackathon 2025

The Cardano Blockchain Hackathon 2025 (Hanoi, Vietnam) has successfully concluded, attracting a large number of talented teams from leading universities and top programmers in Vietnam. After the preliminary round, 15 excellent teams were selected to the final round, where they presented innovative ideas to apply Cardano Blockchain technology in many fields.

🌟 Groundbreaking projects, bold ideas, and passionate people came together to create a memorable hackathon. And now, let’s congratulate our champions!
